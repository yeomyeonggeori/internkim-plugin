We win by being the one app a café owner actually keeps open all day

Positioning

Ledgerline is the bookkeeping app for independent cafés: simple enough to use between customers, sharp enough to run the whole shop on.

General accounting tools treat a café like any small business. We treat it like a café: daily sales, part-time staff, suppliers, and one owner doing everything.

In: daily sales and bookkeeping, cash-flow visibility, tax-ready records.

Becoming in (2027): inventory, payroll, and banking, the rest of the owner's day.

Out: enterprise chains, franchises with central finance teams.

Positioning: bookkeeping app purpose-built for independent cafés. Expansion path is the owner's whole day — inventory, payroll, banking — while staying out of chains and franchises.

Bet 1: inventory sync makes the register and the books agree without the owner typing

Bet 1 · POS inventory sync

Today, café owners re-key sales and stock from their POS into Ledgerline. Syncing inventory and sales data directly from POS systems deletes that work — and makes Ledgerline the place the numbers are trusted.

This is the bet most tied to our positioning: the moment the register feeds the books, leaving Ledgerline means leaving clean data behind.

2027 plan

  • Q1 2027: inventory sync beta with launch POS partners.

  • Beta cohort: existing cafés already on integrated POS systems.

  • Success signal: synced cafés show lower churn and higher engagement.

Bet 1: POS inventory sync. Owners re-key data today; sync removes that. Beta ships Q1 2027 with existing POS-integrated cafés; success measured on churn and engagement.

Bet 2: payroll turns us into the app owners use every week, not just at month-end

2027 plan

  • Q2 2027: payroll pilot with 50 cafés.

  • Scope: schedules, hours, and pay runs for part-time staff.

  • Constraint: payroll rules differ by region — pilot starts where rules are simplest.

Bet 2 · Part-time payroll

Independent cafés live on part-time staff, and payroll is the chore owners fear most. Doing it inside the ledger means hours, sales, and pay live in one place.

Payroll is the widest moat of the three bets: switching cost jumps once we hold pay history. It is also the most regulated — hence the 50-café pilot before any broad launch.

Bet 2: payroll for part-time staff. Q2 2027 pilot with 50 cafés, starting in regions with the simplest rules. Highest switching cost of the three bets, but the most regulated.

Bet 3: automatic bank feeds make reconciliation disappear

Bet 3 · Automatic bank feeds

Importing bank statements is the last manual step in the books. Automatic bank feeds pull transactions straight in, match them to sales, and reconcile in the background.

Closest to GA of the three bets: the plumbing exists, so 2027 is about integration quality and coverage of the banks our cafés actually use.

What changes: statements stop being exported and uploaded; the ledger updates itself daily.

Trust matters most: bank data demands the highest security bar in the product.

GA in Q3 2027, following the inventory sync beta in Q1 and the payroll pilot in Q2.

Bet 3: automatic bank feeds. Removes the last manual step in bookkeeping. Targets GA in Q3 2027; security bar is the highest of the three bets.

Two risks we already know, and the decisions we need from this room

POS vendors may limit API access

Inventory sync depends on partner APIs. We mitigate with multiple POS partners and a direct-integration fallback if any vendor restricts access.

Payroll rules differ by region

Every region has its own withholding, insurance, and filing rules. We mitigate with the 50-café pilot in the simplest regions first and regional compliance review before any rollout.

Decisions for this offsite

  • Approve the 2027 roadmap as sequenced: sync beta Q1, payroll pilot Q2, bank feeds GA Q3.

  • Staff the pilots: POS integrations and regional payroll compliance are the two hiring priorities.

  • Confirm success metrics for each bet so the Q4 pricing review stands on adoption data.
